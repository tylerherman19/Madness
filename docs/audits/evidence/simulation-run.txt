== Scope and method notes
- Covered: steps 1-7 plus round 1 day 2 (top-ups, lock, scheduled auto-assignment, exactly-once). NOT covered in this run: step 8 (rounds 2 to the championship, shrinking survivors, one-survivor and all-eliminated behaviour, "winner" wording search) and step 9 (/admin/operations render). The script builds the whole bracket (scripts/sim/world.mjs) but the later-round driver was not written within the time available.
- 100 entries registered over HTTP (bcrypt 12); 900 were inserted by SQL (bcrypt cost 4) because single-process bcrypt made 1,000 real signups slow. All 1,000 signed in over HTTP.
- Time: the real clock is used. The day being played is "today"; the schedule (slate dates, tip times, locks, fake ESPN feed) is slid by direct SQL (replica role) and the feed file between scheduler calls. The workers were invoked through /api/cron/worker with the interval gate (operation_runs.next_attempt_at) cleared first; every call was required to report claimed:true. The 20 syncs used GET /api/cron/sync-results.
- Assignment runs in batches of 50 per worker call (by design); the script loops until done.
- The pre-lock privacy probe checks home, /api/sweat, /api/live-scores, /grid, /api/grid/export, /api/me, /sweat, /standings as anonymous and as another entry for the target's id (JSON endpoints) and its team near its name; before the check the pages were fresh (picks had just been saved, ISR/caches are revalidated by pick writes).
